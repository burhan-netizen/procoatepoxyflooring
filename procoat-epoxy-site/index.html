What areas does Procoat service?

Procoat is based in Townsville and promotes service across the surrounding area, including Magnetic Island. Ask about your suburb and site access when requesting a quote.

What kinds of spaces can be coated?

Services are listed for garages, sheds, patios and outdoor areas, plus commercial and industrial spaces. The suitable system depends on the concrete and how the space is used.

Can I choose a colour or flake finish?

Colour and flake selections are advertised. Share a photo of your space and the look you have in mind so available options can be discussed.

How do I get an accurate quote?

Send the suburb, approximate floor size, a few photos and any notes about the surface or intended use. Procoat can follow up about the site and scope.

How long does installation and curing take?

Timing depends on the surface, selected system, project size and weather. Confirm the schedule and when the floor can be walked or driven on before booking.

Is epoxy right for every outdoor or wet area?

Not every coating suits every exposure. Ask about slip resistance, UV exposure, moisture and maintenance for your specific site before choosing a finish.
